Original Curriculum. Level 2. Unit 7.

Listen to the story.

Max and Mum visit a market. "What would you like?" asks the seller. "Some tomatoes, please," says Max. The tomatoes cost twenty-two dollars. Mum also chooses bread and eggs. Max checks their list before they pay. They do not buy fish today. The seller gives them a bag, and Max says thank you. At home, they count the eggs together.
