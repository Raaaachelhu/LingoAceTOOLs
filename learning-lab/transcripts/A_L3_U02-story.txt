Original Curriculum. Level 3. Unit 2.

Listen to the story.

Max and Melinda tidy their shared playroom. A blue blanket is on the table, and two pillows are on the floor. "Whose pillows are these?" asks Max. "They are mine," says Melinda. She puts them on the sofa. Max puts his dirty shoes in a box to clean later. They keep a clear path to the door. A tidy room gives them more space to play.
