Original Curriculum. Level 2. Unit 4.

Listen to the story.

At the zoo, Max watches a monkey. It likes bananas. A parrot sits on a branch nearby. "I like parrots. They are smart," says Melinda. Max likes the tiger, but he stays behind the visitor line. He does not touch or feed the animals. The children draw one animal each and describe its body. They watch quietly so the animals have space.
