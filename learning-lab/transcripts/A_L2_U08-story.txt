Original Curriculum. Level 2. Unit 8.

Listen to the story.

Mia and Dad are waiting for a bus. They want to visit Grandma. A man is driving a car nearby. Another person is riding a bike. "I'd like to travel by train next time," says Mia. Their bus arrives. Dad checks the number before they get on. Mia sits down and looks out of the window. Their journey has begun.
