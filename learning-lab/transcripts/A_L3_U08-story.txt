Original Curriculum. Level 3. Unit 8.

Listen to the story.

At an aquarium, Mia watches a turtle swimming. Its hard shell covers much of its body. Nearby, a penguin is standing on a rock. Ben notices its feathers and flippers. The children compare the animals using a display. They do not tap on the glass. A guide explains that animals have different needs. Mia writes one question about where sea turtles live in the wild.
