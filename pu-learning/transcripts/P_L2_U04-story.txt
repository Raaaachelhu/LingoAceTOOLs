Power Up. Level 2. Unit 4.

Listen to the story.

Sam visits his cousin Eva in a tall apartment building. Eva's home is on the first floor, one level above the ground floor. They use the stairs today. Eva's bedroom is smaller than Sam's, but her balcony is bigger. Two model houses are on her desk. The red one is Sam's, and the blue one is Eva's. "Mine has a taller roof," says Sam. "But yours has more windows," says Eva. They build a third house together. It is not the biggest model, but there is space for both their ideas.
