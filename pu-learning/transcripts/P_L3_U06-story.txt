Power Up. Level 3. Unit 6.

Listen to the story.

Year Three organised a classroom celebration. Instead of choosing only the fastest learner, they discussed different kinds of progress. In science, Maya had learned to record results carefully. In history, Theo had started asking questions about evidence. Their teacher said they should explain each award with a real example. The group should not give prizes only to close friends. One pupil suggested a teamwork award for the geography project. Another suggested an improvement award for revising a story after feedback. The children wrote short speeches and checked that every compliment was specific. At the celebration, they discovered that useful feedback describes an action people can repeat, not a label that tells them they are simply clever.
