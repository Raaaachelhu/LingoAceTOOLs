Power Up. Level 2. Unit 9.

Listen to the story.

Ravi moved to a new town last month. At first, finding his classroom was more difficult than it was at his old school. He felt frightened when he had to speak to new classmates. Then a girl called Jess invited him to an art club. Ravi thought drawing a giant town map was more exciting than drawing alone. His group added parks, bridges and colourful houses. The map was not perfect, but everyone contributed. At the school exhibition, Ravi felt proud. The biggest change was not the new town. It was feeling ready to ask for help and join in.
