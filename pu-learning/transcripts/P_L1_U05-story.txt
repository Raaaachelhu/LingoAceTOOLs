Power Up. Level 1. Unit 5.

Listen to the story.

It is May's birthday. Her brother gives her a red kite. Her friend Ben gives her a card. May wants a ball too. There is a blue ball by the door. "Whose ball is this?" she asks. "It is Ben's ball," says Mum. May does not take it. She asks Ben to play together. They fly the kite in the park. May likes sharing her birthday fun.
