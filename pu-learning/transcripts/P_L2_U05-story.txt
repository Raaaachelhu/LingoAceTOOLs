Power Up. Level 2. Unit 5.

Listen to the story.

A wildlife club is making a model animal park. The children use toys, not real animals. A toy whale is the longest animal in their model. A parrot sits above a panda on a paper tree. The penguins are opposite the dolphins. Noor puts a rabbit near a rock. The group checks that every animal has a place to rest. They also make a sign: "Protect animals in their natural homes." At the exhibition, visitors can compare the models and learn why wild animals need safe habitats, clean water and space.
