Listen to the story.

Yesterday, the weather changed three times. In the morning, it was sunny and warm. There were children in the park. At midday, there were dark clouds above the trees. The wind was strong, and soon it was rainy. Mina and her dad were in a cafe when the rain started. They waited safely inside. In the afternoon, there was a rainbow above the houses. Mina drew the three kinds of weather in her diary. Her coat was wet, but her notebook was dry inside her bag.
