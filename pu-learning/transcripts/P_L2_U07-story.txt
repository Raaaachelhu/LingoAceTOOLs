Power Up. Level 2. Unit 7.

Listen to the story.

Last Sunday, Daniel cooked lunch with his aunt. First, they washed carrots and cut them into small pieces. His aunt used the sharp knife and the hot cooker. Daniel measured cold water and stirred the sauce with help. They boiled pasta and made vegetable soup. Daniel did not add cheese because his cousin did not want any. They shared the meal in four bowls. After lunch, Daniel wrote the recipe in his notebook. He learned that cooking includes reading instructions, measuring carefully and cleaning up, not just eating the finished food.
