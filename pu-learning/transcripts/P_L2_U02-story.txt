Power Up. Level 2. Unit 2.

Listen to the story.

Omar has a busy week. He goes to a book club on Monday. He usually swims on Wednesday, but sometimes the pool is closed. Then he reads at home. On Friday, he feeds his neighbour's rabbit. At the pool, everyone must walk beside the water. They must not run. Omar never takes food into the pool. On Sunday, he checks his schedule with Dad. They leave some time for rest. Omar learns that a good week includes school, helpful jobs, hobbies and quiet time.
