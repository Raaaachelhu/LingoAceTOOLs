Power Up. Level 2. Unit 3.

Listen to the story.

Lena is having a costume party. She is wearing a pirate's hat, and her brother is dressed as a farmer. Their cousin Jo is wearing a doctor's coat. Lena hides a paper treasure map near the door. The guests follow its clues around the garden. They find a box of handmade cards under a chair. Jo laughs because one card has a dancing carrot on it. No one needs to buy an expensive costume. At the end, each guest explains a costume choice. Lena says that pretending together is the best part of the party.
