Power Up. Level 1. Unit 6.

Listen to the story.

Leo and Mum go to a park by bus. There is a shop next to the bus stop. There are two tall trees by the gate. Leo has a small map. They walk over a bridge. There are red flowers near the water. There is not a train in the park. "Let's draw our journey," says Mum. Leo draws a bus, a bridge and two trees. It is a lovely day out.
