Power Up. Level 3. Unit 8.

Listen to the story.

A family is planning a city visit for Grandma's birthday. They are going to start at the station and walk past the museum. Then they are going to go through a wide gate into the riverside garden. The shortest route crosses a bridge with steep steps, so they choose a longer route which is easier for Grandma to use. Aisha marks places to rest on the map. Her brother checks the weather and opening times. They are not going to rush through every attraction. Instead, each person chooses one place and explains why it matters. Their guide includes clear directions, rest stops and an alternative plan for rain. A good city break works for the people taking it.
