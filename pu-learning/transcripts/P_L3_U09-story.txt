Power Up. Level 3. Unit 9.

Listen to the story.

Before the hiking trip, Sam checked his backpack with an adult. He packed water, a coat and a simple map. When the group reached the meeting point, the guide explained the route and the weather plan. Sam was excited because the landscape looked different from his town. After they crossed a small bridge, they stopped at a marked rest area. The walk was tiring, so they drank water and rested before continuing. Sam found the compass interesting, but he did not leave the group to test it alone. When they returned, everyone shared one discovery and one useful preparation. Sam wrote that an exciting journey is better when people respect the place, follow the plan and look after one another.
