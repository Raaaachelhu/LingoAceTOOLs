Power Up. Level 1. Unit 4.

Listen to the story.

Mia and Ben have a picnic. Mia likes bananas, but Ben likes mangoes. They have bread, chicken and salad. Ben is thirsty. "Can I have some water, please?" he asks. Mia gives him water. "Would you like some cake?" she asks. "No, thank you," says Ben. He wants a banana now. They put their rubbish in a bag. The park is clean after their picnic.
