Original Curriculum. Level 3. Unit 7.

Listen to the story.

In the morning, the sky is cloudy. Mia wants to play outside, so Dad checks the forecast with her. Soon it becomes rainy and windy. They stay inside and draw pictures of the weather. In the afternoon, the rain stops. They put on coats and take a short walk with Dad. Mia carries an umbrella just in case. Their plan changes when the weather changes.
