Original Curriculum. Level 2. Unit 3.

Listen to the story.

Max and Melinda are at the beach with Mum. A big kite is in a tree. Their red balls are on the sand. "Let's make a sandcastle," says Max. "Good idea!" says Melinda. Mum takes a picture of them. Later, they sit in the shade and read a book. Before leaving, they put their rubbish in a bag. The beach is clean.
