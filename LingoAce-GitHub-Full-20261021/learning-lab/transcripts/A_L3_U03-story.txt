Original Curriculum. Level 3. Unit 3.

Listen to the story.

Our class has a sports afternoon. Mia likes badminton because she enjoys quick games. Ben likes basketball, but he thinks serving in tennis is difficult. Their teacher lets them try slowly before playing a match. Mia misses a shot and tries again. Ben encourages her. At the end, they discuss one skill they improved. Winning is exciting, but helping a teammate matters too.
