Max has a blue bag. A book and a pencil are in it. Melinda has a red pencil case. "Is this your eraser?" asks Max. "Yes, it is. Thank you!" says Melinda. Mili says, "Open your book, please." Max opens his book. Then he passes a pencil to Melinda. They share and learn together.
