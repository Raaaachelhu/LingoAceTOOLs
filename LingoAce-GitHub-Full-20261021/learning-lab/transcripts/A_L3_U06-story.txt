Original Curriculum. Level 3. Unit 6.

Listen to the story.

Ben wants to build a tree house tomorrow. Dad explains that they need an adult and a safe plan. "What should we do today?" asks Ben. Mia suggests reading comic books in the garden instead. They agree to help Mum water the plants first. In the afternoon, they draw a model tree house on paper. A good plan includes permission, preparation and another idea if the first one cannot happen.
