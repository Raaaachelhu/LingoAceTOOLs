Original Curriculum. Level 3. Unit 4.

Listen to the story.

Mia likes science because she enjoys learning about plants. Ben likes art best. He says math is difficult, so Mia helps him draw groups of objects. The picture makes one problem easier to understand. In return, Ben shows Mia how to label a plant clearly. They learn that being good at a subject can grow with practice. Different classmates can help in different ways.
