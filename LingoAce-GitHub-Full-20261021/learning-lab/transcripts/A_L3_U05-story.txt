Listen to the story.

Mia brings a family drawing to class. There are five people in her home: her parents, her little brother, her grandmother and Mia. Her uncle and aunt live in another town. She calls them on Sunday. Ben is an only child, but he has three cousins. The friends compare their drawings. Their families have different numbers of people, and both drawings show people who care for one another.
