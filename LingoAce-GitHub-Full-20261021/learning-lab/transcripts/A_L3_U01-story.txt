Original Curriculum. Level 3. Unit 1.

Listen to the story.

Mia draws an imaginary creature. It has a long neck, curly hair and sharp teeth. Ben thinks it looks frightening. Mia adds a smile and a small book. "It is not angry. It is tired," she explains. Ben draws a place where it can rest. The friends notice that the same face can tell different stories. They ask how someone feels instead of guessing only from appearance.
