Power Up. Level 3. Unit 5.

Listen to the story.

The drama club needed a sea scene for its school show. Buying new props was expensive, so the children searched their recycling box. "Shall we make waves from blue paper?" asked Ada. "We could use cardboard for the boat," suggested Finn. Their teacher helped them check that the materials were safe. The mask was made of thick paper, and the boat was made of reused cardboard. A thin paper handle tore during rehearsal. Instead of giving up, the group added a stronger cardboard strip. They tested each prop before the performance. The audience noticed the colourful scene, but the children were proudest of how they had listened to suggestions and improved their design together.
