Power Up. Level 3. Unit 7.

Listen to the story.

The class interviewed people about their work. Eva spoke to an engineer who designs safer bridges. "What is she like?" asked her partner. Eva explained that the engineer was patient and curious. "What does she look like?" was a different question: she had short hair and wore glasses. The engineer said that when a design fails a test, her team studies the results. If a bridge model bends too much, they change its structure and test it again. Eva had thought engineers always knew the answer immediately. Now she understood that asking questions and working responsibly were important skills. For her dream-job poster, she wrote about the habits she could practise now, not just the job title she liked.
