Power Up. Level 1. Unit 9.

Listen to the story.

May is packing for a holiday. Mum says, "Put your hat in the bag." May adds her shorts and sunglasses. At the beach, Ben enjoys making a sandcastle. "So do I," says May. They make a tall tower together. They do not take shells home. They leave them on the sand. Before lunch, they wash their hands. May draws the sandcastle on a card for Grandma.
