Power Up. Level 1. Unit 3.

Listen to the story.

Ben and May are on a farm. They see a brown horse. It has got a long tail. Two ducks are in a pond. A white goat is next to the barn. It has got short ears. May wants to touch the goat. The farmer says, "Be gentle." Ben helps to feed the chickens. They wash their hands before lunch. The animals are happy, and the children are happy too.
