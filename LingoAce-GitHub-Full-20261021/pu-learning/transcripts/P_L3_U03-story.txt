Power Up. Level 3. Unit 3.

Listen to the story.

The school walking team planned a gentle trail. Before they began, their teacher asked how everyone felt. Max said his knee hurt, so he did not try to prove he was as fast as his friends. He told the teacher and rested with another adult. The others warmed up and carried water. On the trail, they noticed that a longer step was not always better than a steady step. When they returned, Max helped record the team's observations. He wanted to join the next walk after getting appropriate help. The team learned that looking after a healthy body includes speaking up, resting when needed and choosing suitable activity, rather than winning every challenge.
