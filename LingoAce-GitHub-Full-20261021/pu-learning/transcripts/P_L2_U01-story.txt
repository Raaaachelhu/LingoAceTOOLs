Power Up. Level 2. Unit 1.

Listen to the story.

Every Saturday, Nina visits her uncle's farm. She gets up at seven o'clock and gets dressed before breakfast. At eight, she helps her uncle carry water to the animals. A small lake is behind the field. Today, two ducks are swimming there. Nina sees a yellow leaf beside a rock. She draws it in her notebook instead of picking it up. At ten o'clock, she walks to the forest with her uncle. They listen to the birds and return for lunch. Nina's notebook helps her remember the different places on the farm.
