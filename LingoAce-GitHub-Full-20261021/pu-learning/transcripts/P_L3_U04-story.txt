Power Up. Level 3. Unit 4.

Listen to the story.

A nature group followed a marked trail near a waterfall. Their guide walked slowly because the stones were wet. Niko usually walked faster than his sister, but today he moved more carefully. The group stopped when they saw small footprints in the mud. They did not chase the animal. Instead, they observed quietly from the path. A bird flew across the sky, and the children sketched its wings. At the end of the walk, they compared notes rather than collecting plants. The guide explained that good visitors notice details and leave habitats undisturbed. Niko decided that moving slowly had helped him discover more than rushing to the waterfall would have done.
