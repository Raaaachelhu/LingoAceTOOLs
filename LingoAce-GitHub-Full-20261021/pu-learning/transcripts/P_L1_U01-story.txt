Power Up. Level 1. Unit 1.

Listen to the story.

Mia is in a new classroom. Her bag is blue. Her book is on the desk. Two pencils are in her bag. A ruler is under her chair. Ben cannot find his pencil. Mia gives him one pencil. They sit next to the window. The teacher smiles. Now Ben can draw a house. Mia draws a tree. They share their things and make a picture together.
