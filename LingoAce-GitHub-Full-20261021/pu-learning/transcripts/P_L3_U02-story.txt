Power Up. Level 3. Unit 2.

Listen to the story.

Our class made a breakfast museum with drawings and family stories. Mei described noodles which her grandfather prepares on Sundays. Her grandfather is the person who taught her to stir the sauce. Lucas drew pancakes with strawberries. He explained that his kitchen is the place where his family shares weekend news. When the display was ready, visitors compared the ingredients. They noticed that breakfasts can look different and still bring people together. One visitor could not eat milk products, so the children added clear ingredient labels. No breakfast was chosen as the only correct one. Instead, every family contributed an idea, and the class learned to ask questions respectfully.
