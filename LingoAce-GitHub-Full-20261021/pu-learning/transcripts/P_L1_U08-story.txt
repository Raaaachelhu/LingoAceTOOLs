Power Up. Level 1. Unit 8.

Listen to the story.

Mia shows Ben her home. There is a yellow lamp behind the sofa. A small rug is in front of the armchair. The kitchen is next to the living room. Mia can sing, but she cannot play the piano. Ben can play the piano. They make a song together. Mum listens from the kitchen. Later, Ben draws two chairs. He draws a little table between them. Mia puts the picture on the wall.
