Power Up. Level 1. Unit 7.

Listen to the story.

Ben and Mia are in a sports club. Ben is playing football. Mia is throwing a ball to May. May is catching it. Leo wants to ride his bike inside. "Can I ride here?" he asks. "No, you can't. Ride outside," says the teacher. Leo puts on his helmet. After the games, the children take turns playing a guitar. Everyone has a different skill. Everyone can join in.
