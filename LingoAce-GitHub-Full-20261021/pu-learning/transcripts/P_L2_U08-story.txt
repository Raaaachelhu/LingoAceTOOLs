Power Up. Level 2. Unit 8.

Listen to the story.

Yesterday, Aya and her father took a train into town. Aya bought a ticket with her saved pocket money. At the station, she found a map beside the information desk. First, they went to the library to return a book. They had to speak quietly there. Then they walked across the square to the cinema. Aya saw a sign showing that visitors had to keep their phones silent. On the way home, she lost her small map, but she remembered the route. She knew how to reach the station safely.
