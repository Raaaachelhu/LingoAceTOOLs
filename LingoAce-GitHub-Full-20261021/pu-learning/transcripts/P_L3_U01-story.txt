Power Up. Level 3. Unit 1.

Listen to the story.

A school circus club is preparing a short show without animals. Each group writes a practice schedule. Ella begins at half past eight on Saturday because the hall is quiet then. When she was four, she could hop on one foot, but she could not skip. Now she practises skipping for ten minutes and balancing for five. Her friend Amir records the time instead of comparing who is best. After a break at midday, they watch a video of their rehearsal. Ella notices that she looks down too often. She decides to focus on a point on the wall. A small change makes her balance steadier. Practice helps her find solutions, not just repeat mistakes.
